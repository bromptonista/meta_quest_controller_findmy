Field Recovery Playbook (Beating Deep Sleep)

  1. 1
    Wake the Controller's 6-Axis IMU

    Idle Touch Plus controllers enter deep sleep to save battery and turn off their radio. Tap your Quest power button to sleep the headset for 15s and wake it back up, or walk heavily and jostle couch cushions as you enter each room.

  2. 2
    Listen for Wakeup Fanfare & Resonant Chirps

    Keep Passthrough active while sweeping rooms. The instant the controller reconnects, your headset plays a rising 3-note fanfare and immediately hammers all haptic actuators—while keeping speakers quiet between bursts so you can hear the physical buzz.

  3. 3
    Use Cushion-Tap Seismometer & 3D Sighting Pin

    Connected but muffled deep inside a sofa? Pat each cushion and watch the 3D HUD's IMU Seismometer spike when tilted. If Quest's wide-FOV cameras catch even a 1-frame glimpse, a 2m neon pillar stays pinned to that spot in your room.
